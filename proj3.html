About Our College Fest

IGNITE 2026 is an exciting annual college festival that brings together students from different departments. It provides a platform to showcase technical skills, cultural talents, creativity and leadership.

Students can participate in coding competitions, project exhibitions, dance performances, singing, quizzes, workshops and many other activities. Our aim is to encourage learning, teamwork, innovation and student participation.

Rules and Regulations

  1. All participants must register before the event.
  2. Students must carry their college ID cards.
  3. Participants should report before the scheduled time.
  4. Maintain discipline throughout the festival.
  5. Follow the instructions given by coordinators.
  6. Use college equipment responsibly.
  7. Plagiarism and unfair practices are not allowed.
  8. Respect all participants and event organizers.
  9. Participants must follow the rules of each event.
  10. The judges' decisions will be final.
